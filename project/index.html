Five ways to get online

Every connection type trades off speed, latency, availability, and price differently. Here's the short version of each — visit the guide for full detail.

Fiber

Light-based signal over glass strands. The fastest, most symmetric, most reliable option where it's built.

↓ 300–2000 Mbps

Cable

Rides the same coaxial line as cable TV. Fast downloads, widely available in cities and suburbs.

↓ 100–1000 Mbps

DSL

Uses existing phone lines. Modest speeds, but dependable and often the cheapest wired option.

↓ 5–100 Mbps

Fixed Wireless

A rooftop antenna talks to a nearby tower. A strong middle ground for areas without cabled service.

↓ 25–300 Mbps

Satellite

Beams data from orbit. Reaches almost anywhere, including the most remote addresses.

↓ 25–220 Mbps
