WDD 131 – Final Project Site Plan

SignalScout

Find your best-fit internet connection

1. Site Name

SignalScout

The name pairs "signal," a direct reference to telecom connectivity, with "scout," which implies searching for and finding the best option. It reads clearly for a general audience while still hinting at the site's technical subject.

Optional domain: signalscout.io

2. Site Purpose

SignalScout helps homeowners and small business owners understand and compare home and business internet connection types, fiber, cable, DSL, fixed wireless, and satellite, explained in plain language. The site offers a short recommendation quiz based on the visitor's location and needs, a glossary of common telecom terms, and a contact page for follow-up questions.

3. Scenarios

  1. I just moved to a rural area outside of town. What internet options will actually work there, and how fast are they really?
  2. My small business needs reliable internet for video calls and cloud backups. Should I choose fiber or fixed wireless?
  3. What's the difference between latency and bandwidth, and why does it matter for online gaming?

4. Color Schema

A warm terracotta-and-olive "field guide" palette was chosen instead of the cold blue tech look most comparison sites default to. It reads as approachable and human while still feeling credible for technical content.

Ink #1F1B16 Headings, body text, header/footer background
Burnt Terracotta #9A4020 Links, primary buttons, active states
Moss Olive #5B6B4F Secondary accent, badges, hover states
Parchment Cream #F6F1E7 Page background
Paper White #FFFDF8 Card and content backgrounds
